# MCP specification — versioning page and revision changelogs

Pages read on 2026-09-27:

- <https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/versioning> — revision states
  (Draft, Current, Final) and which revision is current.
- <https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-06-18/changelog>
- <https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/changelog>
- <https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/changelog>

## What it says

- Revisions are identified by the date of the last backwards-incompatible
  change. A **Current** revision may still receive backwards-compatible edits;
  **Final** revisions do not change.
- The versioning page named **2026-07-28** as the current revision when read.
- Each changelog lists major and minor changes relative to the previous
  revision; the summaries used here are in [[mcp-latest-revision-2025-06-18]],
  [[mcp-latest-revision-2025-11-25]] and [[mcp-latest-revision-2026-07-28]].

## What we took from it

Only the revision identifiers and the authorization- and transport-related
changes that affect how a remote MCP server is built. Everything else on these
pages is out of scope for this wiki.
